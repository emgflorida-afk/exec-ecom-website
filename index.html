The process / 01—03

Less scattered attention.
More considered action.

Bring the idea, the conditions and the risk into the same conversation. A clear workflow from the first watchlist to the final review.

01 / BEFORE THE MOVE

Start with a plan.

Review technical setups alongside the news and catalysts. Know the level that matters, the entry condition and where the idea stops making sense.

02 / AS IT DEVELOPS

Keep the context in view.

Follow price structure, gamma estimates and changing conditions. Check the exact option, current premium and planned risk before a ticket.

03 / AFTER THE DECISION

Learn from the path.

Revisit how the saved plan developed. Keep signal movement, actual fills and the trading journal distinct so the review stays honest.
